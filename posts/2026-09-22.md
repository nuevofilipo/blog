### Youngest Known Planet Confirmed: Still Being Born

Astronomers confirmed Elias 2-24 b, a Jupiter-sized planet less than one million years old — essentially a "live birth" in planetary science. It challenges existing models of how quickly gas giants can form. The takeaway for your thinking: even in astrophysics, the timeline assumptions experts relied on for decades can be wrong by orders of magnitude. What timelines in tech are we getting wrong?
