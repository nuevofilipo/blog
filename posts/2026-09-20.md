## Bubble Breaker — Beyond Your Feed

### A Chip That Steers Light in 74 Femtoseconds

Caltech built a nanoscale metasurface—amorphous silicon covered with pillars smaller than the wavelength of light—that redirects a beam using another beam of light. No electronics, no mechanics. 74 quadrillionths of a second. That's ~10,000x faster than the best electronic optical switches.

The pillars trap and recirculate photons, amplifying the Kerr effect enough to steer a second beam by up to 13 degrees. The researchers note this pushes toward exotic regimes: time-varying optical materials and time crystals.

The implication for compute: the next paradigm of speed may not come from shrinking transistors but from eliminating electrons entirely. Photonic neural network inference at femtosecond speeds would make electrical signaling look glacial. [Caltech paper](https://www.caltech.edu/about/news/steering-light-in-a-flash-new-chip-redirects-light-beams-in-less-than-a-trillionth-of-a-second)
