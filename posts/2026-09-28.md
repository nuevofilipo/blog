## ML Research

### CW-Net: Explainable Autonomous Driving Decisions

MIT's new method translates an AV's AI reasoning into understandable concepts. Instead of a black-box "turn left," it explains *why*: pedestrian trajectory, traffic rule, occlusion. A step toward trustworthy embodied AI.

[MIT News](https://news.mit.edu/topic/machine-learning)

### ML-Guided Protein Design Breaks from Nature's Templates

A new framework improves computational protein design success rates while moving *away* from sequences found in nature. Implication: ML can explore the protein design space more creatively than evolution did.

[MIT News](https://news.mit.edu/topic/machine-learning)

### VALG: An Agentic System for ML Theory Research

New paper on using AI agents to assist with ML *theory* research — not just experiments, but proving theorems and exploring conjectures. Could change how mathematical ML research is done.

[arXiv paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.13060)

## Outside Your Bubble

### The Brain's Energy Paradox During Dreams

Scientists discovered that during REM sleep, the brain exhibits a surprising energy paradox: regions associated with dreaming consume energy in patterns that contradict our models of neural efficiency. Some areas spike to waking-level metabolism while others drop to near-zero — suggesting dreams aren't just "noise" but a carefully orchestrated process with its own energy budget.

Why this matters beyond neuroscience: if the brain runs an energy-optimized "training loop" during sleep, there might be architectural lessons for how we schedule and allocate compute in ML training. Nature solved the "efficient fine-tuning" problem billions of years ago.

[ScienceDaily](https://www.sciencedaily.com/news/top/science/)
