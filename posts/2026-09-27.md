### Laya Decision Engine — Non-Autoregressive System 1 Decisions

A typed decision engine that provides choice, score, and yes/no decisions over any text in a single forward pass, across 100+ languages. The MLX runtime variant runs 7–14ms decisions on M3 Max with no text generation, no PyTorch, no cloud API. Think about what you could build if classification/routing decisions were near-free and ran locally.

### The "Physical AI" Startup Playbook

With $18.8B flowing into robotics in 2026 alone, there's a pattern emerging: investors want embodied AI with real-world interaction. The gap isn't in building robots — it's in the middleware. Think: perception pipelines, sim-to-real transfer tools, robot fleet management, safety certification platforms. If you're at ETH with robotics lab access, you're sitting on unfair advantages most founders would kill for.

[Crunchbase analysis](https://news.crunchbase.com/robotics/startup-venture-funding-surges-2026-data/)

### Sereact's Cortex 2.0: What "Robot Brains as a Service" Looks Like

Sereact isn't building robots — they're building the AI layer that makes existing robots smart. Their Cortex 2.0 deploys across logistics, manufacturing, and retail. This "brain layer" approach means lower hardware risk and faster iteration. A model worth studying if you're thinking about robotics startups without the capital intensity.

## Outside Your Bubble

### Stanford Records First Real-Time Quantum Jumps of Sound

Stanford researchers have recorded the first real-time quantum jumps of sound — watching single phonons abruptly vanish from one energy state to another. This is the acoustic equivalent of watching Schrödinger's cat decide. Why it matters beyond physics: if we can control quantum states of sound, it opens a path to quantum sensors that work through vibration rather than light — think mechanical quantum computing, or quantum-enhanced accelerometers for robotics navigation in GPS-denied environments.

[ScienceDaily](https://www.sciencedaily.com/)
