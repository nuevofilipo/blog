## "Shai-Hulud" — AI coding assistants as an attack vector

Mandiant disclosed a supply-chain breach where an attacker hijacked an AI coding assistant session and used it to propagate a worm across ~100 repositories of a SaaS provider. The worm spreads through AI coding tools (Claude Code, VS Code agents) as persistence and propagation vectors. Variants targeting npm packages and CI pipelines have also been documented. Worth understanding if you use any AI coding tools.

[The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/attacker-hijacks-ai-coding-assistant.html) · [SonarSource](https://www.sonarsource.com/blog/mini-shai-hulud-targets-ai-coding-agents/)Sep 8–12, unfolding

## OpenAI claims AI-generated Navier-Stokes proof — Clay Institute skeptical

OpenAI says its AI produced a proof of finite-time blowup for Navier-Stokes existence and smoothness — one of the seven $1M Clay Millennium Prize Problems. The Clay Institute says it's not ready to accept it. Mathematicians are questioning formal verification completeness, priority (human collaborators vs. AI credit), and whether the specific formulation actually resolves the Millennium statement. Prediction markets have acceptance at 24–38%.

[Quanta](https://www.quantamagazine.org/ai-has-solved-one-of-maths-1-million-millennium-prize-problems-20260908/) · [MIT Tech Review](https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/09/08/1143747/what-openais-latest-controversy-tells-us-about-the-future-of-math/) · [Scientific American](https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/openai-claims-blockbuster-math-breakthrough-amid-swirl-of-controversy/)Sep 10
