*2026-09-29*

## Robotics & ML

### Helix 2.5 operates autonomously in 30 unseen homes

The latest sim-to-real transfer benchmark: Helix 2.5 successfully completed household tasks across 30 homes it had never seen during training. Digit 5 now lifts 22.7 kg. Skild's S1 foundation model continues to advance generalist robot control.

[Turing Post roundup](https://www.turingpost.com/p/the-latest-breakthroughs-in-robotics-september-2026)

### MIT's tiny flyer: 450% speed boost via learned control

MIT's insect-scale flying robot uses a new AI control system to move with insect-like agility, pulling off 10 somersaults in 11 seconds. The approach is pure learned policy, no explicit dynamics model.

[ScienceDaily Robotics](https://www.sciencedaily.com/news/computers_math/robotics/)

### The shift: from programming robots to training them

Generative AI and multimodal intelligence are making Moravec's Paradox tractable. Imitation learning and RL on massive human-movement datasets are replacing hand-coded controllers. OpenAI is hiring for 19 robotics roles to build humanoids and other form factors.

[Deep dive](https://science-technology.news-articles.net/content/2026/09/04/from-programming-to-learning-the-ai-shift-in-robotics.html)

## Idea Seeds

### D-Robotics raises $400M Series C for robotics infrastructure

Shenzhen-based. They're building the picks-and-shovels layer for robotics: the compute and software infra that every robot company needs. Led by Mirae Asset, with Meituan participating. The "infrastructure for robotics" thesis is hot.

$400M Series C[Funding details](https://techstartups.com/2026/09/17/startup-funding-news-today-september-17-2026-d-robotics-treble-tusk-ic-more/)

### Treble Technologies — acoustic simulation for physical AI

Reykjavík-based, $18M Series A-2. They generate synthetic acoustic data so robots and autonomous systems can be trained on sound, not just vision. Niche, but listen: audio is the least-exploited modality in embodied AI.

$18M Series A-2

### AIDIN Robotics — tactile sensing for robots

South Korea. Strategic investment from HD Hyundai Robotics and Samsung. Building the "skin" for robots: high-resolution tactile sensors. If robots are going to handle fragile objects or work alongside humans, touch matters.

### nanochat by Andrej Karpathy

The full LLM pipeline in one readable codebase: tokenization, pretraining, finetuning, evaluation, inference, and chat UI. If you want to understand how the whole stack fits together, this is the repo to study.

[Trending repos Sept 25](https://github.com/marc-ko/daily-trending-repo/issues/560)

### Laya — non-autoregressive decision engine

System 1 decisions over text in a single forward pass, 100+ languages, 7–14ms on M3 Max. No text generation, no cloud API. Think: typed choice, scoring, and yes/no decisions at the edge. Could be interesting for real-time robot decision-making.

## Bubble Breaker

### Scientists discover a hidden "immune organ" inside the skull

Researchers found a previously unknown structure inside the skull that acts as a rapid first responder against brain cancer in mice. Separately, scientists reconstructed 10-second video clips from mouse brain activity alone — only from signals in the visual cortex. The brain-computer interface implications are profound: if you can decode video from neural signals, the gap between biological and silicon intelligence narrows in both directions.

Why this matters beyond your bubble: the most disruptive technologies often come from unexpected intersections. Neuroscience breakthroughs like neural decoding could reshape how we think about robot learning — what if robots could learn from human neural recordings, not just demonstrations?

[ScienceDaily](https://www.sciencedaily.com/news/top/science/)
