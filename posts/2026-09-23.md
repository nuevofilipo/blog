## ML & AI

### OpenAI Ships GPT-6 Sol and Luna, Halves API Costs

Two new models derived from the flagship GPT-6 Astra. Sol is the everyday workhorse at $2/$10 per million tokens; Luna is the budget option at $0.10/$0.50. OpenAI claims Sol makes half as many mistakes as GPT-5.6 Sol across coding, factuality, and alignment. Both are already in GitHub Copilot.

[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/22/openai-launches-gpt-6-sol-and-luna/) · [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/openai-releases-gpt-6-sol-and-luna-models-slashing-api-costs-50-or-more/)

### xAI Releases Grok 4.7 — 2.1T Params Trained on SpaceX Data

The 2.1 trillion parameter model (up 40% from Grok 4.6) is priced at $2/$6 per million tokens. The technical angle: xAI folded in SpaceX supplemental training data — Starlink telemetry, manufacturing records, engineering failure logs — claiming it reasons better about hardware and physical systems than purely internet-text-trained models.

[GitHub Blog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-09-21-grok-4-7-is-now-available-in-github-copilot/) · [Yahoo Tech](https://tech.yahoo.com/ai/gemini/articles/xai-launches-grok-4-7-171603280.html)

### Xiaomi Open-Sources MiMo-V2.6: 1T Parameter Model Under MIT

Omnimodal model with 1.02T total parameters (~42B active per token via MoE with 384 routed experts), plus Flash and UltraSpeed variants. Released under the permissive MIT license — a significant milestone for open-weight models at the trillion-parameter scale.

[SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/09/22/xiaomi-introduces-mimo-v2-6-series-open-source-ai-model-family/) · [CellCog](https://cellcog.ai/blog/mimo-v2-6/)

### MIT's Insect-Sized Flyer Gets 450% Faster With New AI Controller

New AI control system enables MIT's micro-scale flying robot to perform 10 somersaults in 11 seconds. Roughly the size of a microcassette, lighter than a paperclip, using soft artificial muscles for flapping wings. Targets search-and-rescue in spaces too small for conventional drones.

[ScienceDaily](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/09/260921081114.htm) · [SciTechDaily](https://scitechdaily.com/mits-tiny-flying-robot-just-learned-to-move-like-a-real-insect/)

### NVIDIA Isaac GR00T Open Models for Robot Language Understanding

New open models enabling robots to understand natural language instructions and perform complex multi-step tasks via vision-language-action reasoning. Part of NVIDIA's broader "Physical AI" push toward foundation models for robotics.

[NVIDIA Blog](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/national-robotics-week-2026/)

### Seeed reBot-DevArm: Fully Open-Source Robotic Arm

6+1 DoF desktop arm under CERN-OHL-W 2.0 license. Integrates with ROS 1/2, Hugging Face LeRobot, NVIDIA Isaac Sim, MuJoCo, and GR00T N1.7 out of the box. Under 0.2mm repeat accuracy, up to 2.5kg payload — lowers embodied AI research from lab-grade equipment to a desktop setup.

[GitHub](https://github.com/Seeed-Projects/reBot-DevArm)

## Idea Seeds

### Google AX — Kubernetes for AI Agents

Open-sourced September 18. A runtime to compose, coordinate, and supervise autonomous AI agents across cloud, edge, and on-device using a declarative API. Treats agents as stateful actors with sub-second suspend/resume. 150K+ developer registrations in 48 hours, community Rust/Go bindings already shipping.

[GitHub](https://github.com/google/ax) · [InfoQ](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/09/google-ax-orchestrator/)

### video-use — Edit Video With Coding Agents

From the browser-use team. Drop raw footage in a folder, describe what you want in natural language, get a finished edit. The LLM reads word-level transcripts with timestamps (ElevenLabs Scribe), proposes a strategy, and generates FFmpeg commands. 26K+ GitHub stars. A compelling example of turning a coding agent into a domain-specific tool.

[GitHub](https://github.com/browser-use/video-use)

### microduck\_rl — Open-Source Sim-to-Real RL for Bipedal Robot

From Pollen Robotics. A complete sim-to-real reinforcement learning pipeline for Microduck, a ~25cm bipedal duck robot. Provides MuJoCo Warp training with PPO, realistic actuator physics, domain randomization, and ONNX export. Community forks already teaching it backflips.

[GitHub](https://github.com/pollen-robotics/microduck_rl)

## Outside Your Bubble

### Why Do Fossil Shells Flip Their Spirals Every Few Millennia?

For millions of years, foraminifera — tiny marine plankton in every ocean — have built their spiral shells in one direction, then suddenly reversed globally, only to flip back later. A synthesis of five decades of studies spanning 56 million years found synchronized flips across multiple ocean basins without obvious environmental pressure. The mystery may reveal a rarely observed evolutionary process where a seemingly neutral trait oscillates in lockstep across an entire species' range.

[Quanta Magazine](https://www.quantamagazine.org/why-do-these-fossil-shells-flip-their-spirals-every-few-millennia-20260911/)
