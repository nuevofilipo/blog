## Outside Your Bubble

### Ancient antibiotics, resurrected

Scientists resurrected antimicrobial peptides from mammals that lived millions of years ago and found some were *more potent* against drug-resistant bacteria than modern human versions. The approach — mining evolutionary history for chemical solutions — is a kind of "biological archaeology" that treats deep time as an untapped library. It's a reminder that optimization doesn't always move forward: sometimes the best solutions already existed and were lost. Worth thinking about whenever you default to building something new vs. recovering something old.

[sciencedaily](https://www.sciencedaily.com/)
